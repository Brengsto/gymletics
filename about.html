Why Gymletics

Why train with us?

There are plenty of gyms. Here are six reasons our members stay – and actually reach their goals.

Premium equipment

Over 120 stations from leading brands, serviced regularly and upgraded every year – no waiting for a free machine.

Real coaches

Certified coaches on the floor all day – onboarding, a personal training plan and technique checks are included.

40+ weekly classes

From HIIT and spinning to yoga – every class is part of your membership at no extra cost.

Long opening hours

Open from 6 AM to 11 PM on weekdays and all weekend – training that fits your life, not the other way around.

Wellness included

Sauna, steam room and a quiet lounge for perfect recovery after your workout.

Strong community

Challenges, events and an atmosphere where beginners and pros both feel at home.

Your start

Personal support from day one

We don't just hand you a key card and leave you on your own. We guide you step by step.

  • A personal training plan built around your goals
  • Progress check-ins every 8 weeks
  • Nutrition tips from our coaches
The space

19,000 ft² built for training

Every zone has its purpose – so you always have room to focus on your workout.

  • Free weights area with 8 power racks
  • Cardio zone with 40+ machines
  • Two class studios and a spinning room
  • Sauna, steam room and recovery lounge
