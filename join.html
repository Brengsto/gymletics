FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel during the first 6 months?

No. Memberships using the 20% newsletter discount have a minimum term of 6 months and cannot be cancelled during that period. After 6 months, the regular monthly fee and our standard cancellation terms apply.

What happens after the 6 months?

Your membership continues at the regular monthly price of the plan you chose – €29.90 for Basic or €39.90 for Premium.

Can I unsubscribe from the newsletter?

Yes, any time with one click. Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not affect your membership or your discount.

Can I try the gym before signing up?

Absolutely. Grab a Day Pass at reception, or book a free intro session with one of our coaches.
